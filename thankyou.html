What happens next

1
Check your email from Toastmasters International
You'll receive a welcome email from Toastmasters International shortly. It contains your login details for toastmasters.org — keep it handy.
2
Log in and choose your Pathways learning path
Pathways is the Toastmasters education programme — a self-paced series of projects that build your speaking and leadership skills. Log in at toastmasters.org/login and pick the path that interests you most. There's no wrong choice.
3
Complete your Ice Breaker speech
Your first Pathways project is the Ice Breaker — a short speech about yourself. It's a great way to introduce yourself to the club and get your first speech under your belt.
4
Come along to your first meeting
No preparation needed for your first visit. Just turn up, watch how a meeting runs, and meet the club. We meet on the 2nd and 4th Thursday of the month — see the date above.
