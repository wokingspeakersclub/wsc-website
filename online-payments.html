Where this stands today

Tested and working. Not yet started.

This isn't a leap into the unknown — we've already proved the mechanism works. We just haven't started the actual rollout. But first, it's worth being clear about what a new member has to do today.

Anyone who verbally commits to "pay later" by cash or bank transfer can simply not get round to it — there's no hard number for this club specifically, but it's a well-known soft spot in any "pay me back" process, and step 4 of the current journey depends entirely on it.

The new journey isn't hypothetical — it's already been tested.

We've tested it for three sample months, and it works end-to-end.

How
The Join page calculates the exact fee for whichever month someone joins in. For three test months, it's been wired up to a real Stripe payment page — card, Apple Pay or Google Pay, confirmed instantly.
Why
Purely to prove the mechanism holds together, end to end.
Status
Nothing has been announced or offered to a real new joiner yet.

The business case

What online payment actually buys the club.

Not just convenience — less admin, less risk, and a better first impression at the exact moment someone decides to join.

⚡

Pay the moment they decide

Someone who's decided to join can pay in the next 30 seconds instead of "I'll sort you out next meeting" — which is exactly the moment a sign-up is most likely to be lost.

👀

No more chasing payments

Today, the VP Membership often has to follow up with "did you ever pay that?" and has no real visibility into who's actually settled up. Stripe confirms payment the moment it happens, so there's nothing to chase and nothing left to wonder about.

🧾

Less admin for the Treasurer

No more matching cash envelopes or bank transfer references to names by hand — Stripe records who paid, how much, and when, automatically.

✅

Instant, automatic confirmation

Payment triggers an automatic receipt straight away, confirming to the new member that they're in — no waiting for someone to notice and reply.

📱

Apple Pay & Google Pay included

Younger and more tech-comfortable joiners increasingly don't carry cards or cash at all — a tap-to-pay option removes a real barrier for them.

🏛️

Looks like a serious, modern club

A polished, instant checkout is one more small signal that this club is well run and worth joining.

What it costs

The financial implications, in full.

Stripe takes a small percentage of each payment — nothing more. Figures below are Stripe's standard published UK rates as of today.

1.5% + 20p per UK card payment. No monthly fee, no setup fee, no cost if nobody pays online. Stripe is pay-as-you-go — the club only ever pays a fee on money that actually comes in.

Illustrative annual estimate for this proposal: the ask here is new-joiner payments only, across all twelve months — with every new joiner expected to pay this way from launch. With somewhere around 6–8 new members a year, total fees would come to roughly £15–£20 a year. That's the entire financial cost of the rollout described above. (Extending the same option to six-monthly renewals later would be a separate decision, and a separate, larger number — worth its own proposal if it comes up.)

What this would take

A small, low-risk piece of admin — not a project.

The mechanism is already tested and proven — starting it for real just means working through these five steps, in order.

1

Create the club's Stripe profile

Set up a proper Stripe account in the club's name, rather than an individual's, with payouts going straight into the club's existing bank account — not a personal one. At least the Treasurer and VP Membership get dashboard login access alongside whoever sets it up, so it isn't tied to one person.

Owner: Rameez
2

Set up the payment links for each month

Twelve Stripe Payment Links, one per month, each for that month's pro-rated joining total — a five-minute task per link in the Stripe dashboard, no code involved. This is a small annual task, not a one-off: whoever holds it needs to recreate the twelve links each year prices change.

Owner: Rameez
3

Create the sharing methods

A shareable link and a QR code for each payment link, so it's easy to hand out at meetings, print on materials, or send directly to an enquirer.

Owner: Rameez
4

Agree who reconciles it

Name, by role, who checks the Stripe dashboard against the membership list — most likely the Treasurer, since they already have dashboard access from step 1. A lighter job than today's cash/bank-transfer chasing, but it needs a named owner before launch, not after.

Owner: Committee
5

Start offering it to new members

Nothing has been offered to an actual new member yet — this is the step where it goes from "tested" to "in use."

Owner: Anyone
The case in short

Proven to work. Ready to start.

A three-month test has already shown the mechanism works end-to-end — nothing left to prove. Starting it for real, for new joiners, costs the club roughly 1.5–2% of whatever gets paid through it — likely £15–£20 a year in total — in exchange for faster sign-ups, less cash handling, and one less thing for a volunteer Treasurer to chase.

Recommendation: approve starting online payment for new joiners.
