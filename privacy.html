Woking Speakers Club

Privacy Policy

Woking Speakers Club ("we", "us", "our") is a Toastmasters International-affiliated public speaking club based in Woking, Surrey. This page explains what personal data we collect through this website, why, and what your rights are.

What we collect

When you use our contact form, we collect your first name, last name, email address, and any message you choose to send us, including which meeting date you'd like to attend if you select that option. This is submitted via Netlify Forms and stored in our Netlify dashboard so we can reply to you.

When you join as a member via our Join page, payment is handled entirely by Stripe. We do not see or store your card details — Stripe processes the transaction under its own privacy policy. We retain only what's needed for membership administration: your name, email, and payment date.

Why we collect it

  • To respond to enquiries about visiting or joining the club
  • To arrange your first guest visit
  • To process and administer membership payments
  • To keep in touch with members about meetings and club business

Cookies and analytics

This site does not currently use analytics, tracking, or marketing cookies. If that changes in future, this policy will be updated accordingly.

Who we share data with

We use Netlify to host this site and process contact form submissions, and Stripe to process membership payments. If you join the club, your name and contact details are also held by Toastmasters International for membership and Pathways education records, separately from this website, under their own privacy terms.

We do not sell or share your data with anyone else.

How long we keep it

Contact form enquiries are kept only as long as needed to respond to you. Membership records are kept for as long as you're a member, plus a reasonable period afterwards for club administration.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR, you can ask us what personal data we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. To do any of these, email us at info@wokingspeakers.org.uk and we'll get back to you promptly.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page.
