A proposal for the committee

Should we switch to the new website?

A side-by-side look at wokingspeakers.org.uk today vs. the proposed replacement — checked live, not just prettier.

First impression

Same club, better presentation.

This is what a guest actually sees in the first two seconds on each site — nothing staged, no cherry-picking.

Old site

The first thing you see on landing is a website that looks dated, with no real hook for a new visitor. The hero photo is nice, but it carries no headline and no message of its own — a first-time visitor learns nothing about what to expect.

New site

A modern design that presents the club at its best. The headline speaks directly to a nervous first-timer, with the club's free, no-pressure offer right underneath — and the next meeting date is visible before you've even scrolled.

Same club, not a rebrand. Same name, same logo, same meeting details, same committee — this is a clearer presentation of Woking Speakers Club, not a change of identity. Guests and members will recognise it immediately.
What's improved

Everything that already existed, done better.

Same club, same information — but rebuilt around the person who actually matters most: a guest deciding whether to walk through the door.

1

A more modern design

OldDated template look, generic globe logo repeated three times in the header.
NewA custom typeface pairing and a warm navy/gold palette designed specifically for this club.
2

Better mobile friendly

OldTwo competing frameworks (Bootstrap + Materialize) fight over layout — menus and text can break on phones.
NewBuilt mobile-first from day one, on a single stylesheet, and tested on the devices guests actually browse with.
3

Improved navigation structure

Old"Meetings" opens a 4-item submenu (Venue, A Typical Meeting, Calendar, Toastmaster Tips) — the date is 2 clicks deep, on the Calendar page.
NewEverything lives on one page — About, Meetings, First visit, FAQs, Join, Stories — reachable instantly from the menu, no page loads.
4

Focused on the main audience

OldReads like it was written for existing members, not for someone deciding whether to come at all.
NewThe headline, "First visit" section and FAQs are all written directly to a nervous first-time guest.
5

Next meeting date in a banner

OldNothing about the next meeting appears on the homepage at all.
NewA sticky banner on every page shows the date, time, and a live countdown ("In 9 days"), one click from "come along."
6

Upcoming meetings, not a calendar

OldYou have to click into a calendar widget and browse month by month to find a date.
NewA plain list of the next several dates, each with a one-click "add to calendar" button for Google, Apple or Outlook.
7

Clear call-to-action buttons

Old"How Do I Join?" sits in the nav bar with the same weight as "Links" or "FAQs" — easy to miss entirely.
NewTwo obvious buttons sit in the hero itself — a primary, free invitation, and a lower-pressure "see what happens."
8

A much better membership section

OldA single "How do I join" page with no price breakdown.
NewA dedicated section with a clear 3-step process and a transparent price box — joining fee, monthly cost, and free guest visits, spelled out.
What's genuinely new

Things the old site never had at all.

Not improvements on existing pages — these simply don't exist on wokingspeakers.org.uk today.

🧮

Automated fee calculator

The Join page works out your total cost on the spot based on your start month, alongside a clear list of member benefits — no manual maths for the Treasurer or the new member.

💳

Online payment

Joining fees and renewals can be paid online via Stripe — no more chasing cash or cheques at a meeting.

✅

A proper thank-you page

After joining or getting in touch, visitors land on a real confirmation page instead of being left wondering if anything happened.

🧭

A custom 404 page

Broken or mistyped links land on an on-brand page that helps people find their way back, instead of a bare server error.

🔒

A privacy page

A clear, GDPR-aware explanation of what data is collected through the contact form and Stripe, and why — something the old site has never had.

💬

Testimonials & member stories

Real before/after stories from members, plus a "who comes here" section — giving prospective guests social proof the old site never offered.

How easy is this to launch?

Nothing new to learn, nothing to migrate.

This isn't a mockup or a prototype — every section on this page is already built and working. Going live is a small, low-risk step, not a project.

1

Already built and tested

The whole site is finished and running today — nothing to design, code, or approve from scratch.

2

No new tools for the committee

Meeting dates, prices and wording are edited by opening a plain text file and typing the change — no admin panel or software to learn.

3

Enquiries and payments already wired up

The contact form and online membership payments work out of the box — nothing further to set up after launch.

4

Going live is a single step

Pointing wokingspeakers.org.uk at the new site is the only change needed — nothing for members to install or learn.

The case in short

A first impression built to turn visitors into guests, and guests into members.

A prospective guest lands on a clearer pitch, finds the next meeting date instantly instead of digging through a submenu, and gets two obvious buttons inviting them in — not a nav-bar link buried among ten others. Every change on this page is aimed at the same goal: more guests walking through the door, and more of them coming back.

Recommendation: approve switching wokingspeakers.org.uk to the new site.
